I began my career at TBWA London, working on Sony PlayStation, Absolut Vodka, Nissan and Müller yogurts.

Following a move to T&P, I have spent the last 16 years leading integrated teams — originally in the London office as business leader, head of account management, then partner, before moving to Canada as the agency launched there. During that time I’ve been fortunate to work on our Lexus Europe, Lexus Global, Toyota Canada, GlaxoSmithKline and TELUS businesses.

For the last decade in Canada I have helped design, build and run the integrated team that manages the national TELUS business across creative, strategy and media. Most recently I oversee ‘The Showroom’, our dedicated team for Toyota Canada, running creative and media across their national footprint.

If you need to find me at the weekend, I’ll either be slowly dragging myself around a Gran Fondo or Ironman course — or, more likely, in a bakery eating something glazed.
